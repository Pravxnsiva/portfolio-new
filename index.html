About

Where design decisions meet backend logic.

A quick spec sheet on how I work and what I'm currently focused on.

I'm a Master of Computer Applications candidate with a genuine pull toward both sides of a product: the interface people touch, and the system that makes it work. My day-to-day runs on the MERN stack — MongoDB, Express, React, and Node — supported by JavaScript, Python, and Java for whatever the problem calls for.

I like starting projects in Figma before I write a line of code, which keeps the interfaces I ship intentional rather than accidental. Lately I've been pulling generative AI and NLP into that process — voice interfaces, prompt-driven features, and web assistants that respond to more than clicks.

Outside of coursework, I'm active with the GRD Developers Club and Google Developer Group Coimbatore, and I'm open to opportunities across India and abroad.
